How It Works

11 PM, NH-37 Assam. A passing driver, Ravi, stops at a crash scene.

🗹0:00Ravi taps SOS on PWA — or sends "SOS" on WhatsApp
🤖0:05GPS locked. AI asks triage questions in Assamese (phone locale detected)
🚑0:20HIGH severity → Ambulance, nearest hospital, police, towing & puncture shops dispatched
📋0:35Voice + AR overlay guides Ravi through bleeding control while stabilizing neck
📸1:10Scene photos uploaded. Police alerted. Victim's ICE contact called via NFC tag
🏥11:20Ambulance arrives. ER pre-warned with triage notes. The Golden Hour is intact.
